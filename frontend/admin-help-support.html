Welcome, Admin!

Here to help with any questions.

🔍

Managing Users

Open Manage Users, find the user's row, and use the status action to deactivate them, or the remove action to take their account down entirely.

Use the ✉ Mail action on their row in Manage Users. It sends a message straight to their notifications — useful after reviewing a complaint against them.

Registrations

Open Inspect Registration, review their submitted NIC and supporting documents, then approve or reject from there.

It moves to the Rejected tab in History, and the applicant is notified. They're free to re-apply with corrected details.

Reported Conflicts

Open Reported Conflicts and select Review on any row to see the full heading, description, and any proof the reporter submitted.

Select Complete on that row once you've resolved it. It then appears under Resolved Conflicts in History.

Dashboard & Notifications

It plots new registrations per role, per week. Use the period dropdown to switch between this month, last month, the last 3 months, or a custom date range.

Notifications are generated by real activity — a new report or registration. If nothing's happened since your last check, there's simply nothing new yet.

Account & Security

Contact your technical team directly — the admin account is managed separately from regular user accounts and isn't covered by the self-service reset flow.
