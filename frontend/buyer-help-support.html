How can we help?

Search our buyer FAQs below, or reach out to our support team directly.

🔍

Finding Crops

Use Search for Crops and filter by crop type or district. Each listing shows the farmer's name and location so you know exactly where your order is coming from.

Listings disappear once a farmer sells out or removes them. Try checking back later, or search for a similar crop from another farmer nearby.

Orders & Payments

Open a crop listing, choose your quantity, and confirm the order. The farmer is notified immediately and will accept or reject it from their side.

You can cancel from My Orders as long as it's still Pending. Once a farmer accepts it, message them directly if you need to change or cancel it.

Payment details are arranged directly with the farmer once your order is accepted — check their listing or message them for their preferred method.

Delivery

You'll get a notification the moment the farmer marks your order as Delivered. You can also check the status any time from My Orders.

Open the order and select Received to confirm delivery. This lets the farmer know everything arrived and completes the order.

Don't mark it as received yet. Message the farmer with details first, and file a report from Report Issues if it isn't resolved.

Messages & Notifications

A Message button appears on any accepted order. You can also open Messages directly to see every conversation in one place.

Check the bell icon at the top of any page — it updates the moment something happens on your account, like an order being accepted or delivered.

Reporting a Problem

Go to Report Issues, choose the category that fits, and describe what happened. Add the order ID if you have one — it helps our team find the details faster.

An admin reviews it and may reach out to the farmer directly. You'll see the status update in Report Issues once it's resolved.

Account & Security

Open Settings from the sidebar to update your phone number and delivery address. Your name and NIC can't be changed here — contact support if either needs correcting.

Select Forgot password? on the login screen and follow the reset steps sent to your registered email.
