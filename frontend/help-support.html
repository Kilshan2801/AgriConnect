Getting Started

Go to My Crops and select Add New Crop. Add a photo, harvest date, quantity, and price per kg. Your listing appears to buyers as soon as you save it.

Click your name in the top-right corner. Every role registered to your account is listed there, with your current role checked. Select another role to switch into it.

Orders & Payments

Open Orders to see every order a buyer has placed. Each pending order has Accept and Reject buttons. Once accepted, mark it Delivered when the crop is on its way.

Your Total Sales figure updates once the buyer confirms they've received the order. If a buyer hasn't confirmed after a few days, message them directly from the order row.

Message the buyer first to check in. If the issue isn't resolved, file a report from Report Issues with the order ID so our team can step in.

Equipment Rental

Browse Equipment Rental and send a rental request for the dates you need. You'll get a notification once the provider approves it, and can message them any time to arrange pickup.

Don't mark the rental as received. Message the provider with photos first, then file a report from Report Issues if it isn't resolved.

Messages & Notifications

Check the bell icon at the top of any page — notifications appear there the moment something happens on your account, like a new order or an accepted rental. Nothing to set up on your end.

A Message button appears on any order or rental once it's accepted. You can also open Messages directly to see every conversation in one place.

Reporting a Problem

Go to Report Issues, choose the category that fits (order, rental, or user), and describe what happened. Add the order or rental ID if you have one — it helps our team find the details faster.

An admin reviews it and may reach out to the other user directly. You'll see the status update in Report Issues once it's resolved.

Account & Security

Open Settings from the sidebar to update your phone number, address, and district. Your name and NIC can't be changed here — contact support if either of those needs correcting.

Select Forgot password? on the login screen and follow the reset steps sent to your registered email.
